AXI4-Lite & Simplified AMBA AXI profile: five channels, no bursts, no transaction IDs. \\
BHT & Branch history table; here the direction bit inside the predictor entry. \\
BTB & Branch target buffer; here the tag and target inside the same entry. \\
Claim & The moment the CPU commits to a handler, signalled to the PIC by \sig{cpu\_irq\_ack}. \\
CSR & Control and status register, accessed by the CSR instructions rather than over a bus. \\
DECERR & AXI decode error response, produced by the interconnect for an unmapped address. \\
EOI & End of interrupt; signalled by \sig{cpu\_irq\_eoi} on an interrupt-returning \sig{MRET}. \\
Effective band & The band a PIC source is using now, which differs from its configured band after an escalation. \\
Hart & Hardware thread; one CPU instance here. \\
IF/DX, DX/WB & The two pipeline registers, between S1--S2 and S2--S3. \\
In service & A PIC source that has been claimed and sits on the nesting stack. \\
LSU & Load/store unit; the data-side AXI master. \\
M-mode & RISC-V machine mode, the only privilege level implemented. \\
PIC & Programmable interrupt controller, \file{hdl/pic.v}. \\
Pending & A PIC source with an enabled request that has not yet been claimed. \\
RAS & Return-address stack. \\
Redirect & The signal that changes the fetch path: misprediction, trap entry or \sig{MRET}. \\
S1, S2, S3 & The three pipeline stages: fetch, decode+execute, writeback. \\
SLVERR & AXI slave error response, produced by a real slave that refuses an access. \\
Spurious claim & A claim whose source had already deasserted when the acknowledgement arrived. \\
SVA & SystemVerilog Assertions; the concurrent-property layer in \file{debug/sva/}. \\
WARL & Write-any, read-legal: a field that accepts a write and returns a legal value. \\
WSTRB & AXI write byte strobes, one bit per byte lane. \\
